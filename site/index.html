Play Together: your friends, on your PS5

Send a link and friends join the game you're playing as Player 2, 3 and 4, each with their own controller. They need no PS5 and no app: any browser on a phone, tablet or computer will do.

  1. 1

    Open Play Together

    While you play, press the people button on the Play bar (on a phone, ⋯ › Play Together). Share the QR code, the link or a short code like K7M-2QX.

  2. 2

    Your friend scans or opens it

    The game opens in their browser, in their language: they type a name and you press Allow when it pops up over the picture. With the PS5Upload app, the invite opens straight into the game.

  3. 3

    They play as Player 2 to 4

    On the TV they press PS and pick Quick Play. Each friend is a separate player with your picture and sound, up to three friends at once.

On a phone, the screen is the controller

A friend on a phone plays full screen with on-screen controls, the buttons out of the way until they press and hold the picture. A controller over USB or Bluetooth works too, and so does the keyboard.

  • The picture stays within about 150 ms of the console, with rumble on their controller
  • The game's sound, with a Mute button that's remembered
  • A Wi-Fi blip doesn't cost them their spot: they're back as the same player within a minute
🌍

Friends far away too

With Tailscale on your computer, the invite offers a Tailscale link that reaches you from anywhere your friend's Tailscale does. WireGuard and other VPNs work too. Nothing to open to the internet.

🛡️

You stay in charge

Nobody plays until you allow them, and you can remove anyone. One switch turns off friends' PS and Create buttons, so a stray press can't pull everyone out of the game.

🔗

Links open in the app

A friend with PS5Upload is asked whether to open the invite in the app, the way Slack and Zoom links do, or join from Play › Join a friend with the link, the code, a QR scan or Playing nearby.

Remote Play, built in

Play is the heart of the app

Your console's screen, streamed to the device in front of you. It wakes the PS5 from rest mode, signs your user in, and you play with a controller, the keyboard or touch.

One press from Home

The Play card sits at the top of Home: press Play, or pick up your last game where you left it. Every game's page has Play too, which starts the game and streams it in one go.

  • Wakes the console and signs in for you, asking for your PS5 passcode if there is one
  • Keeps playing in a mini player while you use other screens, and over other apps on Android
  • Fit, Fill or Stretch the picture; full screen and picture in picture

Set up once, with a checklist that ticks itself

Play › Set up walks through everything a console needs, in order. It reads the console every few seconds and ticks each step as it's done; where the app can do the step, the button is right there.

  1. Connect to your PS5
  2. Sign in to a user and give it an account ID
  3. Sign the account in, offline
  4. Turn Remote Play on
  5. Pair this app with the console, or with the PIN the PS5 shows

Phones as controllers and screens

Scan a code and a phone becomes a controller, or a screen for friends to watch from the couch. Hand a watcher the controller when it's their turn.

  • Phones as controllers, up to four
  • Watch party with sound, up to four watchers
  • Instant replay, screenshots and clips, saved to your phone's photos
  • Friends somewhere else? Play Together gives them their own player from a link

Your keyboard and mouse, as the PS5's own

Play has three ways to use your keyboard and mouse, kept for each game. Switch with the button on the stream bar, or with ⌃ ⌥ M (Ctrl+Alt+M on Windows and Linux).

  • Controller: the keys and mouse act as a controller, as before
  • Keyboard & mouse: the PS5 gets a real keyboard and mouse, for games and apps that support them, the PS5's browser and homebrew such as terminals
  • Mixed: a real mouse, while the keys act as a controller and WASD moves

Click to play, ⌃ ⌥ to get out

Click the picture and the PS5 has your keyboard and mouse. Press and let go of ⌃ ⌥ (Ctrl+Alt on Windows and Linux) to get them back.

  • A card shows the keys the first time, and again when you seem to be looking for the way out
  • A faint "⌃ ⌥ to release" stays at the top while you play
  • Both shortcuts can be changed in Play › Settings › Controls › Shortcuts

A phone as the PS5's touchpad and keyboard

On a phone or tablet, switch the picture from Controller to Touchpad: drag to move the pointer, tap to click, tap with two fingers to right-click and drag with two to scroll. ⌨ opens your phone's keyboard and types on the PS5.

A phone used as a controller has a Touchpad & keyboard tab too.

Everything in one app

Fill it, sort it, keep it

Everything between your devices and your PS5, built on its own fast transfer protocol.

Upload & Install

Drop a package, a game folder, an image or an archive: the app works out what it is and does the right thing. Transfers are resumable and run at the full speed of your network, with thousands of small files packed together so they never crawl.

  • Install from your computer, a link or a network share
  • A queue that carries on while you do other things: pause an upload, or send something else first
  • Updates and add-ons install from each game's page
  • Archives are unpacked, or sent as they are when that's what's in them (payloads, a jailbreak update)

All your games, in one place

One grid for the games on your drives, your network shares and every console, with their updates and add-ons. Each game's page shows where it is and what you can do with it: Play, Launch on PS5, install, move or update.

Saves and files

Back your saves up on a schedule or with one press, restore them, and copy one to another user on the same PS5 or to a friend. Emulator saves (PS1, PS2, Switch) too. Browse the console's drives like a folder on your computer: copy, move and open files both ways.

Workshop

Turn a game folder into a package or a disk image, look inside one, and make images smaller with compression. Everything runs on your computer and goes to the console when it's ready.

  • Pause a conversion and carry on later; add more games to its queue while one is made
  • Use less of this computer: half the processor, at low priority, so it stays quick

On every device you have

Native apps for macOS, Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone, and a Docker image that serves the same app as a web page from a NAS or server. Several consoles? Add them all and switch with a tap.

Guide

Get started in five steps

From download to your first game on screen. Most of it happens once.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Download it for your computer or phone below. On a NAS or server, run the Docker image and open it in a browser instead.

  2. 2

    Load the helper on the PS5

    Start your PS5's payload loader as you usually do, then press Send helper on the Connection screen. The helper is how the app talks to the console.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Type the console's address on the Connection screen, or let the app find it on your network. The status at the bottom turns green when it's there.

  4. 4

    Set up Play

    Open Play › Set up and follow the checklist. Each step ticks by itself when it's done; the one only the console can show (its rest-mode settings) you tick yourself.

  5. 5

    Play, and fill it up

    Press Play on Home. To add a game, drop it on Upload & Install; it shows up in Games with Play ready when it's installed.

How do I…

Play together with a friend
  1. Start Play, then press the people button on the picture's bar (on a phone, ⋯ › Play Together).
  2. Send your friend the link, or let them scan the QR code. Press Allow when their name pops up.
  3. On the TV, they press PS and pick Quick Play (or their own user), then play.

Not on your network? Both of you join the same Tailscale network and pick Tailscale above the QR code. The FAQ has the details (in English).

Play with a phone as the controller
  1. Start Play on your computer.
  2. Press the phone button on the picture's bar.
  3. Scan the code with the phone's camera. The phone opens a controller; turn it sideways.

In the PS5Upload app on a phone, Play shows the computer that's playing: tap Use this device as its controller.

Let friends watch
  1. In the phone panel, choose To watch.
  2. Friends scan the code: the stream plays in their browser, with sound after a tap.
  3. Press Hand the controller next to someone to let them play.
Install a game or an update
  1. Drop the package on Upload & Install, or pick it with Browse.
  2. It joins the queue and installs; you can keep using the app.
  3. For an update or add-on, open the game's page in Games and install it from there.
Back up and restore saves
  1. Open Save Data and choose a folder for backups.
  2. Back up one game, or turn on automatic backups.
  3. To go back, pick a backup and press Restore (close the game first).

While playing, the game panel takes a snapshot of the running game's save in one press.

Make the picture sharper on a big screen
  1. In Play, open Settings › Picture.
  2. Turn on Upscale to the screen, and set Sharpen to taste.

It runs on your graphics card and turns itself off if the device can't keep up.

Use it from a browser (Docker)
  1. Run the Docker image on your NAS or server (the command is below).
  2. Open port 19113 on that machine in a browser.
  3. The whole app is there. Play needs an https:// page, so to play from another computer or a phone, put the engine behind an https proxy or use the desktop or phone app.
Use it from a phone
  1. Install the app: the Android or iPhone download below. The iPhone app is sideloaded with AltStore or Sideloadly and an Apple ID; a free one works.
  2. Open Connection, enter the PS5's address and send the helper. The app talks to the PS5 directly, Play included.
  3. Rather use a browser? Open port 19113 of a computer at home that runs the Docker image, or port 9180 of the PS5 for its own page (first turn on Allow other devices on the network in that page's Settings).

Play in a browser needs an https:// page. Over a plain http:// address, play with the app.

Play away from home
  1. PS5Upload works on your home network. Never open its ports to the internet: it has no password.
  2. Join your home network with a VPN such as Tailscale: on your phone or laptop, plus a computer at home set up as a subnet router, or the ps5-tailscale payload on the PS5.
  3. Connect to the PS5 at its usual address and play. How smooth it is depends on your home connection's upload speed.

Through a VPN, Stream & install and installs from a link don't work (use Upload & install), and waking from rest mode may not: leave the PS5 on.

Wake the PS5 without the TV
  1. The TV turns on with the PS5 because of HDMI-CEC.
  2. On the PS5, turn off Settings › System › HDMI › Enable HDMI Device Link, or turn off CEC on the TV (Anynet+, SIMPLINK, BRAVIA Sync…).
  3. Wake and Play now leave the TV off. The controller won't turn the TV on any more either.
See where Play's delay comes from
  1. Press Stats on the picture's bar (on a phone, in the ⋯ menu).
  2. The line net · queue · decode · screen shows each stage in ms: the network to this device, the wait for the decoder, decoding, and showing the picture.
  3. A big net: a cable for the PS5 and 5 GHz Wi-Fi help most. A big decode: try 720p. Low latency in Play's settings shortens the queue.

The PS5's own part and your TV's delay aren't in these numbers; use the TV's game mode.

Something isn't working
  1. Open Bug Report in the sidebar: it builds a report with the app's logs.
  2. Attach it to an issue on GitHub, or ask on Discord.
  3. Hard to catch? Turn on Detailed logs in the Logs tab, make it happen again, then build the report. It turns itself off after 24 hours.

The in-app Help screen answers most questions. Its FAQ is in English, and it is also on GitHub.

Docker (the app as a web page on your NAS or server)

Serves the app on your network: open port 19113 in a browser. Start from engine/compose.yaml, or change the command's two addresses and its folder to your own. Docker Desktop on a Mac or Windows PC has no host networking; follow the FAQ there.

Let scripts and AI agents drive it

Everything the app does with your PS5 is also an API on your own computer, with an MCP server built in, so an AI agent can check a console, move files, install a game or play through Remote Play.

MCP for AI agents

Works with Claude, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI and other MCP clients. There is nothing extra to install, and it can run read-only.

An HTTP API for scripts

Every route is described in an OpenAPI document the engine serves itself, and a guide covers errors, long calls and events.

It stays yours

There is no password, so by default it answers only the computer it runs on. You choose who else can reach it. Keep it off the internet.
