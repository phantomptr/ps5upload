## 9. Privacy

PS5Upload has no user accounts and collects no usage data (no telemetry, analytics or
tracking). In short:

- **On your devices.** Your games, files, saves, settings, logs, pairing keys and passwords stay
  on your own computers, phones and PS5s. Logs and job records are kept locally; a bug report
  is a file made on your device, with addresses, serials and keys removed, and it goes only
  where you choose to post it.
- **Between your own devices.** The app, engine, helper, Minion and PS5Upload Play talk to each
  other directly on your network, or over Tailscale if you set it up.
- **The Play Rooms hub** (hub.phantomptr.com) only helps people connect over the internet. It
  sees the network address of each device that connects, and what a listed room shows: its name,
  the host's name, the game, the number of players and its settings. It keeps these only in memory, only while they are needed (a room's
  card until the room closes or after 12 hours at most; rate-limit counters for minutes, up to
  an hour), never writes them to disk, never logs addresses, names or messages, and does not
  share them. It never receives chat, voice, video or passwords, and never lists anyone's
  address. Once two devices are connected, they talk to each other directly. The hub's
  [PROTOCOL.md](engine/crates/ps5upload-hub/PROTOCOL.md) has the details.
- **People you play with.** To connect directly, devices exchange their network addresses after
  the host lets someone in. The host sees the names guests choose, and everyone in a room sees
  the names, chat and voice of the others.
- **Other services.** Some features contact other services when you use them: GitHub (update
  checks, release and payload downloads), the payload, cheat and artwork sources you choose,
  Tailscale (if you sign in), public STUN servers (to find a route over the internet), streaming
  services (Go live) and Ko-fi (if you donate). Those services see your network address and
  follow their own terms and privacy policies.
- **The website** sets no cookies and has no analytics. It remembers your language and theme in
  your browser, asks GitHub for the latest release, and its Rooms page asks the hub for the
  room list. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which follows GitHub's own privacy statement.
