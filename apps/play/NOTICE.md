## Code from other projects

- **Native app boilerplate** (`tooling/native/`, `tooling/prospero-clang18`, `tools/make-stub.sh`,
  `tooling/libc.prx.sha256`, much of `tools/build-in-container.sh`, the tiled framebuffer address
  function and the video-out and pad structures): from PS5Term, GPL-3.0-or-later. The native app
  boilerplate is Copyright (C) 2026 BlackBearReloaded.
- **libSceVideodec2 structure layouts and call order** (`src/ps5/sys.hpp`, `src/ps5/video.cpp`):
  follow the PS5 VLC port's `ps5/modules/ps5vdec.c` (GPL-2.0-or-later,
  https://github.com/ZiZc3/VLC).
- **libSceAudioIn call signatures and constants** (`src/ps5/mic.cpp`, `src/ps5/mic.hpp`): our own
  code; the argument order, port types and formats follow the shadPS4 emulator's
  `src/core/libraries/audio/audioin.h` (GPL-2.0-or-later, https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4),
  checked against the call sequences in renan-marcel/PS5_PayloadSDK PR #1 (GPL-3.0-or-later) and
  ThatRetiredDude/TokenStation5 PR #19 (GPL-3.0-or-later). No code was copied.
- **Font baking** (`tools/make-font.py`) and **asset checks** (`tools/assets/check-assets.py`):
  adapted from PS5Term's `tools/generate-font-atlas.py` and `tools/validate-assets.sh`
  (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- **QR Code generator library** (`third_party/qrcodegen/`): Copyright (c) Project Nayuki, MIT
  License (the notice is at the top of each file), v1.8.0.
- **Opus** (libopus 1.5.2, fetched at build time with a pinned SHA-256 and linked into the app):
  Copyright the Xiph.Org Foundation, Skype Limited, Octasic, Jean-Marc Valin, Timothy B.
  Terriberry, CSIRO, Gregory Maxwell, Mark Borgerding and Erik de Castro Lopo; BSD 3-clause
  license (https://opus-codec.org/license/).
- **BearSSL** (the repository's `third_party/bearssl/`, TLS for the hub's `wss://`): Copyright (c)
  2016 Thomas Pornin, MIT License (`third_party/bearssl/LICENSE.txt`). The trust anchors are the
  ISRG roots the PS5Upload helper's tailnet uses (`tailnet/src/isrg_roots.h`).
- **JetBrains Mono** (`third_party/jetbrains-mono/`, the UI font baked into `assets/font.bin`):
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`third_party/jetbrains-mono/OFL.txt`).
- **Noto Sans** SC, TC, JP, KR, Arabic, Devanagari, Bengali and Thai (fetched at build time at
  the pinned commit in `third_party/noto/SOURCE.json`, with SHA-256 checks): the words of
  languages the UI font has no letters for are drawn with them at build time into
  `assets/i18n/*.bin`; the fonts themselves are not shipped. SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (`third_party/noto/*-OFL.txt`). The way those words are made (shaped runs) follows PS5Term's
  `tools/generate-i18n.py` (GPL-3.0-or-later).

## Art and music

- The menu and selection music is an original composition made for PS5Upload Play, generated by
  `tools/assets/make-music.py` (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- The home-screen backgrounds and the in-app background are drawn by `tools/assets/make-art.py`
  around the PS5Upload logo from this repository's `branding/` folder.
- `pic0.dds` and `pic1.dds` are encoded with our own BC7 encoder, `tools/assets/bc7enc.c`
  (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- `snd0.at9` is made at build time by PS5 AT9 Converter
  (https://github.com/BlackBearRevived/ps5-at9-converter, GPL-3.0-or-later), pinned to commit
  74a722898a6b97fd52256770bf45187e40c3fa2e; it is fetched into `.deps/` and is not part of the app.

## Source code

PS5Upload Play's complete source is `apps/play/` in https://github.com/phantomptr/ps5upload, at
the tag of the PS5Upload release this copy came with (`git clone --recursive --branch <tag>`
also fetches the BearSSL submodule). The pinned downloads the build makes (payload SDK,
libopus, Noto fonts, AT9 converter) are named with their hashes in `tools/build-in-container.sh`
and `third_party/*/SOURCE.json`.
